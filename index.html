💡 課程理念 | Educational Philosophy

【用好奇心看世界，用實力說故事】

當孩子完成 Let's Go 的基礎航行後，Bobby 老師將帶領他們跨入更廣闊的知識海洋。在 Advance A1 班，我們不再只是練習單純的對話，而是將美語轉化為「探索世界的望遠鏡」與「表達自我的邏輯腦」。透過 Bobby 老師引領的主題探索課程 (Oxford Discover 1)，孩子將化身小小觀察家，從自然、科學到社區生活，在解決問題的過程中建立獨立思考的習慣；同時，配合 Joy 老師的語法活用引導 (Azar Grammar)，將零散的單字編織成完整的邏輯，讓孩子從「說單字」進步到「講故事」，自信地活用美語寫下對世界的發現。

【Discover Your World, Build Your English】 After mastering the basics, it's time for young learners to use English as a tool to explore something bigger. In our A1 Advance Class, we bridge the gap between simple conversation and meaningful discovery. By integrating inquiry-based exploration with systematic language building, we empower 3rd to 5th graders to become confident thinkers.

🗺️ 本學期的成長藍圖 | Our Learning Journey (34 Lessons)

這學期的 34 堂課是一場精彩的探險！孩子將跟著老師的腳步，完成以下學習里程碑：These 34 lessons represent an exciting adventure! Together with our teachers, students will reach the following learning milestones:

🌍【世界探索篇】用美語看見無限可能

Part 1: World Discovery - Seeing Infinite Possibilities through English (Oxford Discover Units 1-9)

  • 第一站：暖心連線 (Units 1-2) 🏫 —— 從家裡到社區，探索我們與朋友的社交小世界。
    Station 1: Heartfelt Connections - Exploring our social circles, from families to making new friends in the community.
  • 第二站：自然觀察員 (Units 3 & 5) 🌳 —— 走進森林觀察大樹與動物的家，學習尊重生命。
    Station 2: Nature Observers - Venturing into the wild to observe trees and animal habitats, learning to respect all living things.
  • 第三站：色彩實驗室 (Unit 4) 🎨 —— 動手調色，用英文理解科學的因果變化。
    Station 3: The Color Lab - Mixing colors to understand the cause and effect behind basic scientific principles.
  • 第四站：全球小公民 (Units 6-8) 🌍 —— 從世界文化到四季更迭，建立寬廣的國際視野。
    Station 4: Young Global Citizens - Journeying from world cultures to the changing seasons, building a broad international perspective.
  • 第五站：邏輯小尖兵 (Unit 9) 🔢 —— 讓數字不再只是符號，而是生活的應用。
    Station 5: Logic Pioneers - Transforming numbers from abstract symbols into practical tools for daily life.

✍️【語言活用篇】把英文說得更有邏輯

Part 2: Language in Action - Expressing Thoughts with Logic (Azar Grammar Chapters 14-15) 我們將專注於兩個最重要的「表達超能力」：

  • 形容詞魔法 (Chapter 14)：
    學會精準描述事物的樣貌，並練習用「大多數、一些」來表達對數量的觀察，讓句子變得豐富生動。 Adjective Magic (Chapter 14): Learning to describe the world with precision and using quantifiers like "most of" or "some of" to make sentences rich and vivid.
  • 比較級與最高級 (Chapter 15)：
    練習「一樣、相似、不同」的對比，學會操作「最棒的其中之一」等進階句型，讓說話更有說服力。 Comparisons & Superlatives (Chapter 15): Practicing how to contrast "the same," "similar," and "different." Mastering advanced structures like "one of the best" to make speech and writing more persuasive.
